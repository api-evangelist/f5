Enterprises are moving quickly from AI experimentation to AI operations, and that shift changes what organizations need from application delivery infrastructure. For years Kubernetes traffic management was treated as an implementation detail, with platform teams selecting an ingress controller, application teams configuring routes, and security teams adding controls where they could; F5 NGINX Gateway Fabric aims to standardize that delivery.
