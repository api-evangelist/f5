Object storage systems are struggling to handle the increased traffic demands from AI workloads, so F5 BIG-IP now serves as a delivery layer in front of S3-compatible storage clusters. F5 is introducing native S3 support in iRules and publishing deployment guides for integrations with Dell ObjectScale and MinIO AIStor.
